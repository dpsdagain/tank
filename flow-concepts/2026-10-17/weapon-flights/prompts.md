# Flow weapon flight concepts

Provider: Google Flow browser UI, existing user project. Model shown: Nano Banana 2.1. Image mode, 16:9, x1 per request. Flow displayed 0 credits per image. These are concept boards, not production sprite atlases. Neither the built-in image generation tool nor the CLI was used.

## Board 1

Create a premium painted CONCEPT ART BOARD for the projectile flight animations of a 2D side-view arcade tank artillery game. Landscape 16:9, exactly FOUR columns by THREE rows, twelve evenly spaced labeled vignettes. Dark midnight-indigo backdrop, subtle teal atmosphere, no battlefield or interface. Every weapon must have a different, instantly readable silhouette, tactile materials, beautiful volume, sharp highlights and restrained glow. Hand-painted stylized 3D game-art quality, chunky toy-like proportions, crisp side-on orthographic view, matching teal mountains and amber dusk lighting. All projectiles travel left to right; each has a short characteristic trail contained inside its own vignette. Leave generous breathing room. Render the projectile bodies large and detailed, not as tiny glowing dots. Do not make all weapons missiles or fireballs. No generic flat vector icons, no realistic handheld guns, no ground explosions, no text other than the exact twelve weapon names under their vignettes. The labels should be small clean warm-white lettering. This is a visual exploration sheet, not a registered animation atlas. Arrange in this exact reading order. ROW 1: "Single Shot": compact pointed magenta-and-brass shell, a thin pink tracer; "Big Shot": fat blunt red heavy shell with metallic bands and a thick warm wake; "3 Shot": three separate lavender finned darts in a fan; "5 Shot": five distinct violet darts in a wider fan. ROW 2: "Sniper Rifle": a slender polished silver needle with a fine white streak, not a gun; "Cannon Ball": solid dark iron sphere with a rotating-looking reflective highlight and almost no trail; "Chain Reaction": linked copper/orange fuse capsules with a bright spark stepping between sections; "Skipper": flat mint-green edged spinning disc with dotted skim-light. ROW 3: "Roller": compact cyan-rimmed mechanical wheel with visible spokes; "Heavy Roller": broad armored drum with two golden luminous rims, noticeably heavier than Roller; "Napalm": tumbling weathered copper liquid-fire canister leaking glowing orange droplets; "Hail Storm": faceted ice-blue crystal capsule with delicate frost vapor and a few distinct tiny crystal pellets. Keep the exact twelve names, no title and no extra labels.
