# Flow prompt set

Provider: Google Flow, existing user project. Model: Nano Banana 2.1. Output count: x1 for each request. Images were generated through the Flow browser UI, as the user requested; the built-in image generation tool and CLI fallback were not used.

## 1. Battle concept — 16:9

Create a polished art-direction keyframe for a beautiful 2D side-view arcade artillery browser game. Wide 16:9 composition, locked orthographic side-on camera, not an isometric scene. Two small chunky toy-like tanks face each other across a rolling destructible grassy hillside: olive-green tank at the left fifth, coral-red tank at the right fifth. The terrain surface is bright crisp grass over dark layered soil. At right-center a spectacular orange-gold fireball erupts: a white-hot core, overlapping irregular lobes of incandescent flame, dark sculptural smoke curling upward, sprays of glowing embers, flying earth chunks, and a thin expanding ground shockwave. Give the explosion strong readable shape and texture, not a plain glowing circle. Deep midnight indigo sky, distant teal mountain layers, a warm low orange sun, cinematic rim lighting. Premium stylized hand-painted game art with crisp playable silhouettes and rich saturated colors, a little tactile 3D depth but a strict flat side-scrolling camera. Leave the upper third calm for a game HUD. Keep both tank silhouettes readable and the overall landscape broad and uncluttered. No UI, no text, no logos, no borders, no watermark. This is a visual target for a real-time canvas game, not a screenshot of an existing game.

Flow title: **Tanks battling on grassy hillside**. Saved as `battle-concept.jpg`.

## 2. Scenery layer — 16:9

Create one production background layer for a premium stylized 2D side-view artillery game, 16:9 landscape. This asset will sit BEHIND procedurally destructible grass-and-soil terrain and small tanks in a real-time browser game. Paint only an atmospheric midnight-indigo sky and distant layered teal mountain silhouettes, with a small warm amber sun near the upper-right third. Attractive hand-painted illustration with cinematic color and clean graphic shapes, luminous amber haze at the low horizon, subtle drifting cloud streaks and a few very fine stars. The upper third must remain quiet and low-detail for a HUD. The bottom third must be soft distant atmospheric haze and far mountains, without a sharp playable ground line, near rocks, or foreground objects. Rich blue/teal and warm golden accents, clear contrast but not washed-out. Locked orthographic side view. No tank, no weapon, no explosion, no foreground grass or dirt, no characters, no interface, no text, no logo, no watermark. Do not add any scene framing or border. Make this usable as a clean reusable game background rather than a finished gameplay screenshot.

Flow title: **Game background layer creation**. Saved as `scenery-layer.jpg`.

## 3. Explosion atlas — 1:1

Create a precise VFX flipbook SPRITE SHEET for a real-time 2D side-view artillery game. Square image, exactly FOUR columns by FOUR rows, sixteen equal square cells, read left to right then top to bottom. Each cell contains one chronological frame of the SAME orange-gold explosion, centered at the exact same position and scale. Pure solid black background, no floor, no environment, no tank. No visible grid lines, labels, text, numbers or borders. Maintain generous empty gutters so no flame, smoke or spark crosses into a neighboring cell. The sixteen frames show a short fluid sequence: tiny white-gold ignition; white-hot expanding flash; expanding irregular orange fireball; overlapping red-orange flame lobes; peak incandescent explosion with a bright core; rolling flame breaking into dark charcoal smoke; cooling ember-filled plume; smaller fading smoke wisps; last few sparks. Make the effect intensely attractive: sharp white-yellow core, textured curling orange flame, rich red edges, sculptural smoke, scattering tiny sparks. Premium stylized hand-painted game VFX, strong readable shapes, consistent orthographic side-view, no camera or perspective changes. The effect anchor stays in the center of every cell. This must look like a practical evenly registered animation atlas, not a collage of different explosion designs. Keep every frame fully inside its own cell and do not include a checkerboard pattern or fake transparency.

Flow title: **Explosion sprite sheet creation**. Saved as `explosion-sheet.jpg`.
